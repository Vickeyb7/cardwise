## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of high-tier private wealth advisory and rigorous Swiss editorial typography. Designed for sophisticated personal balance sheets, multi-card portfolio rewards arbitrage, and strategic liquidity optimization, it eliminates the gamified, neon tropes of typical fintech apps in favor of composed authority, quiet luxury, and architectural restraint.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Luxury Physicality**:
- **Canvas:** Warm, non-sterile porcelain substrates evoke heavyweight bespoke rag paper and polished limestone.
- **Contrast & Depth:** Razor-sharp typography in deep obsidian and slate anchors the visual hierarchy, offset by warm frosted glass panels and razor-thin hairline boundaries.
- **Accents:** Restrained champagne metallic sheens indicate elevated actions and card tier statuses, while deep forest emerald signals validated yields, surplus earnings, and point redemptions.
- **Physicality:** Digital representations of credit instruments emulate premium titanium, matte obsidian, and fine bullion cards with authentic embossed specular micro-accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an opulent, light-flooded atmosphere anchored by uncompromising structural contrasts.

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Obsidian Slate):** Used for primary buttons, commanding typographic hierarchies, active iconography, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Champagne Gold):** Reserved for elite tier indicators, rewards multiplier highlights, active card status strokes, and metallic visual micro-accents.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E` - Deep Forest Emerald):** Designates verified annual savings, positive net-value yields, optimization recommendations, and favorable monetary deltas.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Deep Mineral Charcoal):** Drives body content, secondary reading levels, and precise metadata labels.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Base canvas: Porcelain Warm Alabaster (`#FBFBFA`)
  - Elevated card surfaces: Pure Warm Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) with secondary tier panels in Soft Ecru (`#F6F5F2`) and recessed wells in Buff Stone (`#EDECE8`).
  - Hairlines: Warm Cashmere Borders (`#E2E0D8` and `#D8D5CD`).

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, tuned to deliver both mechanical clarity in numerical data tables and high-luxury prestige in editorial balances and headlines.

- **Numerics and Monetary Units:** All balance displays, point multipliers, and currency indicators must utilize tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain vertical alignment across asset allocations.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** `label-sm` applies uppercase tracking with `+0.08em` letter spacing for financial tags, tier identifiers, category classifications, and tabular column titles.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** High-magnitude figures (`display-lg`) pair with quiet, subdued subtitles (`body-sm` in `#64748B`) to keep screens uncluttered.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture utilizes a strict, proportional column grid with wide white margins to reflect bespoke private banking literature.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer page margins of 48px (`3rem`) to 80px. Content max-width sits at 1360px to preserve comfortable scan lengths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Sidebars compress to unified icon rails or slide-out ledgers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Multi-column financial metrics convert to horizontal snap-scroll cards or stacked ledger sheets.
- **Rhythm Rules:** Vertical gap cadence strictly observes the 8pt rhythm scale: `space-xs` (4px) for baseline badge-to-label gaps, `space-sm` (8px) for icon-to-text distances, `space-md` (16px) for intra-card padding, `space-lg` (24px) between ledger rows, and `space-xl` (40px) between major analytical sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on heavy synthetic dropshadows, visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm light diffusion**, **layered tonal planes**, and **calibrated hairline precision**.

- **Level 0 (Basebed):** Porcelain Alabaster (`#FBFBFA`) unadorned.
- **Level 1 (Structured Cards & Ledger Rows):** Pure ivory (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a continuous 1px hairline border (`#E2E0D8`) accompanied by a subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Sheets & Action Modals):** Warm ivory surfaces floating over backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), rimmed with a 1px border (`#D8D5CD`) and deep ambient shadow: `0 12px 36px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tactile Micro-Relief (Physical Credit Cards & Badges):** Dual-light edge highlights simulate metallic beveled edges using an inner drop-highlight: `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6), inset 0 -1px 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Gold accents apply subtle directional micro-gradients (champagne `#E4C78D` to `#C5A059`) to emulate cold-stamped foil.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an architectural, tailored feel with an intentional **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry:
- Standard elements, inputs, and analytical tiles use **`0.25rem` (4px)** for clean, tailored edges.
- Container panels, financial summary modules, and dialogue cards utilize **`rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px)**.
- Full card objects (credit cards, visual passbooks) use an intentional **`rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px)** conforming to ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 proportions.
- Circular treatment (`rounded-full`) is strictly quarantined to status pips, avatar portraits, and standalone icon utility buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Obsidian Slate (`#0F172A`) with pure white text, 1px border (`#0F172A`), `0.25rem` radius, and `space-sm` x `space-lg` padding. Hover introduces a subtle lift and gold rim tint: `border-color: #C5A059`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Clear or alabaster background, 1px border (`#D8D5CD`), Obsidian Slate text (`#0F172A`). On hover, transitions to Soft Ecru (`#F6F5F2`) with tightened hairline (`#0F172A`).
- **Tertiary (Optimization Action):** Deep Forest Emerald (`#0F766E`) background with white typography for single-tap reward optimizations and point transfers.

### Physical Tactile Credit Cards
- Visual digital replicas of cards (Black, Platinum, Gold, Reserve) rendered at a 1.586:1 aspect ratio.
- Features embedded micro-textured chip illustrations, debossed cardholder nomenclature via subtle CSS text-shadow (`0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)` on light cards; `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` on dark cards), and metallic hairline perimeter strokes.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Verification Badges:** Soft Emerald tint (`rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.08)`), Forest Emerald text (`#0F766E`), 1px hairline (`rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.2)`). Used for "Optimal Card" or "5x Yield Active".
- **Prestige Tier Tags:** Champagne Gold wash (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.1)`), Champagne Gold text (`#8E6F2F`), 1px gold hairline (`#C5A059`). All badge text styled with `label-sm` uppercase tracking.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Minimalist ledger rows divided by hairline rules (`#E2E0D8`).
- Hover states transition seamlessly into a luminous wash (`#F6F5F2`).
- Monetary figures right-aligned, formatted with `label-lg` in tabular figures (`tnum`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Pristine warm ivory background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#D8D5CD`) and `0.25rem` radius.
- Height standardized to 44px for balanced touch targets.
- Focus state activates an obsidian hairline stroke (`#0F172A`) accompanied by an ambient champagne diffusion ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`).

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- Primary containers feature an opaque ivory face (`#FFFFFF`), a continuous 1px perimeter line (`#E2E0D8`), and internal padding of `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal metrics utilize high-contrast stacked layouts: micro-caption at top (`label-sm`), hero figure centered (`headline-lg`), and yield attribution delta at baseline (`body-sm` in emerald).
